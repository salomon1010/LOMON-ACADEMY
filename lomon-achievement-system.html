Habit Engine · Attendance Tracking

Streak Counter

Click any day dot to toggle attendance. Streaks build automatically. Missing a day breaks the chain — but the system recovers with a grace day rule.

Streak Rules
🔥
Streak counts consecutive Academy Block days attended (Mon–Fri, 4:45–6:15 PM). Weekends do not count for or against the streak.
🛡️
Grace Day: One missed day per week does not break the streak — it freezes it. Two consecutive missed days breaks the chain.
⭐
Milestones: 5-day = First Week badge · 10-day = Two Weeks Strong · 20-day = Month Warrior · 30-day = Iron Scholar · 60-day = Academy Legend.
🔁
Recovery: After a break, a new streak starts immediately. Best streak is always preserved and displayed alongside current streak.
🎯
Tired Day counts: The 60-minute low-energy Academy Block variant counts as a full attendance day. Showing up matters more than session length.

Glory · Grade 7 · Governor's School Pipeline

The Learning Journal That
Becomes Her Portfolio

Every analytical essay, every breakthrough moment, every skill unlocked — documented from Week 1. By Week 12 Glory won't need to build a portfolio. She'll already have one. Governor's School applications open in Grade 10 (2028). She has two years to fill it.

1
The Daily 5-Minute Entry System
Every Academy Block ends with Glory writing one paragraph in her journal — not a summary of what she studied, but a reflection on how she thinks. This is the distinction that makes a portfolio powerful vs generic.

She writes in Google Docs (one doc per week, saved in a "Lomon Academy Journal" folder in Drive). Each entry has a date, a skill learned, and one of the three prompts below. Total time: 5 minutes maximum. The writing prompt from the day often gives her the raw material.
"Today I learned that ___. The reason this matters is ___."
"I used to think ___ about this topic. Now I understand that ___."
"The hardest part today was ___. I got through it by ___."
2
Monthly "Best Of" Curation (10 minutes)
On the last Friday of each month, Glory reads back her daily entries and picks 3 that she's most proud of. She copies those into a "Portfolio Highlights" document and adds one sentence explaining why she chose it.

This teaches her to evaluate her own work — exactly the metacognitive skill Governor's School programs look for. By Week 12 she'll have a curated set of 9–12 highlighted entries.

File structure: Lomon Academy / Portfolio / 2026-May-highlights.doc
3
The Essay Portfolio (4 Graded Pieces)
Each module produces one graded essay. After revision, Glory adds it to her portfolio folder with a cover note she writes herself — one paragraph explaining what the essay is about and what she learned in the process of writing it.

Governor's School applications often ask: "Describe a learning experience that changed how you think." Glory's answer will come directly from these cover notes. She's writing the application in real time, starting today.

Essays saved as: Essay-01-Week3-connotation-analysis.doc · Essay-02-Week6-myth-literary.doc · etc.
4
The "I Taught My Sister" Log
From Week 4, Glory explains one skill per week to Grace or Angely. After each tutoring moment, she writes one sentence in her journal: "This week I taught ___ to ___. The part she understood fastest was ___."

This is leadership evidence. It shows she can teach, communicate, and take responsibility for others' learning. Governor's School programs love this. It also deepens her own mastery — the Feynman Technique in action.
