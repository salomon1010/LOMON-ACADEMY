Instead of: "Stop doing that!"

Try: "I can see you're frustrated. In our family we handle that by ___. Can you try that?"

Instead of punishing behavior, name it and redirect it. The rule is the reference — not your emotion in the moment.
Pillar 4 — Environment Control

Screen Time Rules

WHO/AAP guidelines adapted for your family. You control the inputs your children receive — this shapes their attention, mood, and values more than almost anything else.

Non-negotiable screen rules for all kids
📵 No screens during meals — meals are for conversation and connection
🌙 All devices off 1 hour before bedtime — protects sleep and melatonin
🏫 No screens during Academy Block — learning time is protected time
✅ Homework/chores completed before any recreational screen time
👨‍👩‍👧 You can always see what they're watching — no private screen time in bedrooms

Weekly Family Meeting — Sunday 30 minutes

Weekly Check-in

The single most powerful habit you can build. Every person speaks, every person is heard. Problems surface early. Wins are celebrated. The week ahead gets planned together.

Step 1 · 5 min · Everyone
🌟 What was your best moment this week? Start with the youngest, go around the circle. No interruptions.
Step 2 · 5 min · Everyone
😤 Was there anything hard or unfair this week? Each person gets 60 seconds. No defending — just listening.
Step 3 · 5 min · Kids
🗳️ Family vote topic this week: Use the Vote tab to let kids pick something for the week ahead.
Step 4 · 5 min · Parents only (after kids)
💍 Salomon & Pascaline: What do we need from each other next week? One sentence each — specific and kind.
Step 5 · 5 min · Everyone
📅 What are we looking forward to next week? Each person names one thing. End on a high note.
Salomon's personal reflection · Private
🧘 How am I modeling the behavior I want to see? Am I calm, present, consistent? What do I want to do better next week?
